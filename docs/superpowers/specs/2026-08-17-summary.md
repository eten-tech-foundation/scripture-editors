# Standard view: integration summary

Six parallel tracks (plus the serial Phase 1 settle-loop fix already in the base) merged into
`sv/integration` in both repos on 2026-08-17. This is the one-document account: what changed, what
is verified and how, what each track deliberately deferred, what the merge itself surfaced, and
what needs TJ's decision. The residual backlog — including everything deferred here — is
`2026-08-17-residual-backlog.md`; the by-hand script is `2026-08-17-manual-test-script.md`.

Merged branches: `sv/whitespace`, `sv/char-stack`, `sv/structural-caret`, `sv/attribute-markers`,
`sv/unknown-blocks`, `sv/marker-resolution` (scripture-editors, 82 files, +10,039/-621);
`sv/whitespace-core`, `sv/attribute-markers-core` (paranext-core, 3 files, +544/-7).

## 1. What changed, by track

Each track's own handoff note carries the full account; these are the one-paragraph versions with
the note names.

**Settle-loop safety** (Phase 1, in the base; `2026-08-14-settle-loop-handoff.md`). The
two-keystroke freeze is fixed at its root: glyph-bearing display-run scanners classify by rendered
bytes (`$isCanonicalMarkerNode`, one shared predicate replacing a drift-prone duplicate), and
`$charNodeTransform` no longer merges adjacent char spans that render their own glyphs — the
structure/byte disagreement that made the settle oscillate forever. An 8-deep cascade backstop
logs and halts instead of freezing.

**Whitespace** (`2026-08-14-whitespace-handoff.md`). The USX parser preserves whitespace-only text
nodes by meaning (content iff no line break), closing all four lost rows of invariants §7c —
and C# capture tests prove ParatextData preserves the `\cat*`-trailing space on every leg, so the
user-visible loss was entirely our load leg: **no C# change needed, gate never triggered**. The
trailing-space transforms went from exclusion lists to allowlists (add only before a verse / after
a char span), delete shrank to the empty-verse space, and both §7 fabricators are gone — all five
corpus skip entries deleted. Separator deletion now means what the bytes mean: the
tokenize-identity predicate lives beside the tokenizer, char-opener separators heal O(1) when
identical and re-tokenize when not, para prefixes grace-then-settle on departure. A second
fabricator found along the way (collapsed-note NBSP separators merging into content) is fixed in
both adaptor twins.

**Char-stack** (`2026-08-14-char-stack-handoff.md`). One close-and-reopen primitive
(`$liftOutOfCharStack`, `libs/shared/src/nodes/usj/charStack.utils.ts`) now serves `\fq`-style
note-marker applies, Ctrl+Space (caret and range), multi-line paste, `\fp` breaks, and paragraph
splits through INSERT_PARAGRAPH. Owner-ratified behavior changes: a note-content marker ENDS the
implicit span it is typed inside (no `\ft` reopen); range Ctrl+Space unformats every level and
drops emptied spans (no more fabricated `\nd \nd*` pairs); Enter mid-span continues the reopened
style and the caret lands inside it.

**Structural deletion and caret** (`2026-08-14-structural-caret-handoff.md`). Whole-line deletion
semantics ratified and shipped via provenance arming (`$armWholeParaDeletion` — emptiness alone
never reaps); typing over a glyph-touching selection is delete-then-type; Escape no longer blurs
the caret away (`EscapeKeyPlugin`); the `\v 1\ ` typed-backslash shape gets a Tier-1 arm that
keeps the caret on the typed character; `$unwrapCharNode` reinserts after the span so an
element-point caret survives the same-commit unwrap.

**Attribute markers** (`2026-08-14-attribute-markers-handoff.md`). The C# capture suite grew 7→18
pins (cp-with-markup divergence, cat fold rules, unclosed ca, the whitespace matrix with its
chapter/verse asymmetry). `ATTRIBUTE_MARKERS` and paranext-core's markers map are pinned in
agreement. The chapter settle scope exists (third instance of the pattern), and `\ca`, `\cp`
(inline, TJ's choice), and note `\cat` display and edit with full construct/heal/pend/settle
wiring. A real tokenizer fix: the chapter path consumes one whitespace-only token after a folded
`\ca`, matching ParatextData. The writer's `\ca` newline special case was KEPT and documented
(PT9 Standard View's own byte shape; an earlier removal was reversed by TJ's correction).

**Unknown blocks** (`2026-08-14-unknown-blocks-handoff.md`). Table rows show their `\tr ` glyph
(with the PT9-parity indent cancel); typed-`\fig`/`\esb`/`\tr` at a paragraph end no longer
strand the caret before the box (`$selectAfterSentinelRun`); a keystroke into token-mode opaque
content is refused instead of replacing the whole caption (`OpaqueBlockGuardPlugin`);
`closed="false"` suppresses closing glyphs the file does not contain. The `\fig` graying rule was
reviewed and deliberately kept (a bare `\fig` is genuinely not a figure yet); it is now pinned as
a rule across six shapes.

**Marker resolution** (`2026-08-17-marker-resolution-handoff.md`). Closer-glyph edits pend and
settle on departure instead of resolving under the caret; nested opener renames stay Tier 1 and
carry their closer; `ImmutableUnmatchedNode` is editable TEXT under Invariant I (edit, delete
byte-by-byte, re-match by re-tokenization), token-atomic in non-editable views; typing at a
closer's end splits out immediately; and the live "`\va` after a verse resolves at `\v`" bug is
fixed by merging the verse split's rest into following content plus widening the resolve shield
to the caret's contiguous plain-text run.

## 3. Merge account — conflicts and compositions

The merge was far cleaner than the ownership table predicted. Three textual conflicts total:

1. **`verseAdjacentTyping.test.tsx` (add/add)** — the planned "one shared test" was written
   INDEPENDENTLY by whitespace (byte pins, full plugin trio) and structural-caret (caret pins,
   engine only). Resolution: both suites kept in the one file, headers merged. No behavioral
   tension — each file explicitly declined to assert the other's half.
2. Two one-line import-list conflicts in `markerEditTier1.utils.ts` (marker-resolution and
   attribute-markers each adding an import beside whitespace's). Both kept.

**The contended files composed correctly.** Audited per invariants §8 by diffing each track's tip
against the merged result and accounting for every removed line:

- `markerEditDeletion.utils.ts` (predicted four claimants; actually touched by two).
  Whitespace took `$healMarkerTrailingSeparator` (caret-held deletions grace + pend instead of
  silently healing); structural-caret took `$paraMarkerDeletionTransform` (armed whole-line reap)
  and the `$unwrapCharNode` reorder. Marker-resolution never needed the file (the §8 table
  assigned it `$unwrapCharNode`, but its fix made the unwrap moot on its paths), and char-stack's
  split fix kept it out too — the coordination the invariants demanded happened by tracks
  narrowing their blast radius, not by negotiation.
- `markerEditTier1.utils.ts` (predicted two claimants; actually touched by four). Whitespace:
  separator grace and tokenize-identity routing in the settle dispatch. Marker-resolution: the
  closer/opener resolution arms and the `$resolvePendingMarkers` except-key SET. Structural-caret:
  a new `VERSE_MARKER_REST_REGEX` Tier-1 arm. Attribute-markers: chapter transform + literal-block
  changes. All four sets of hunks are disjoint at function level; nothing was reverted.

**Compositions checked semantically, not just textually:**

- **The two verse rest-extraction arms differ in strategy** (the one real composition wrinkle).
  Marker-resolution's arm merges the extracted rest into a following plain-content text node (its
  mid-typed-literal fix); structural-caret's new no-separator arm creates a fresh node. Not a
  defect today — the widened caret shield covers the caret's contiguous plain-text run either
  way, and Lexical's normalization merges the unstyled fresh node — but the asymmetry is a drift
  hazard: a future edit to one arm's caret handling will not obviously owe the other the same
  change. Recorded in the backlog as a small unification.
- **Char-stack's §6d finding is FIXED by composition**: the paragraph-final Ctrl+Space space was
  eaten by the old trailing-space delete branch; whitespace's rewrite reduced delete to the
  empty-verse space, so the space now survives. The residual is test-only: char-stack's
  "closes the stack without reopening" test still runs on the char-sync-only harness and should
  move to the full harness. In the backlog.
- **Structural-caret ↔ char-stack**: both handoffs record the same reconciliation — the menu
  (Enter-Enter) split still bypasses the close-and-reopen primitive
  (`$splitParagraphWithMarker` calls `selection.insertParagraph()` directly), so the unwrap and
  its caret fix remain load-bearing. Routing the menu split through the primitive is
  structural-caret's work item D, in the backlog with their caret recommendation.
- **Marker-resolution's unmatched-v2 node vs the base's un-skipped delta round-trip** (the
  `6c416eda` un-skip landed after marker-resolution branched): composed green — shared-react
  1535 passing includes that suite.
- The `2sa.lexical.*` fixtures: four tracks regenerated them independently; the merged result is
  regeneration-stable (verified, not assumed).

**No merge resolution had to be corrected** — nothing met the "outright wrong" bar.
